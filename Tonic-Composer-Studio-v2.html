    Mixer

    Levels, panning, mute and solo

    Mixer

    Balance the whole song. Slide a fader for volume. Drag a knob up or down to move a part to the right or left (L and R show where it sits). Mute silences a part, Solo plays it alone. Double-click a control to reset it. Muted parts are left out of downloads too.

    Master

    Press play or start a jam to see the sound.

    Jam

    Play chords live with the band

    Jam

    Tap chords while the band plays. Each change waits for the beat, so you always land in time. Home chords feel settled, Lift opens things up, Tension wants to move on. The melody sits out so you can play or sing over it.

    New chord starts

    Tap to set speed: tap 4 times in time with the music.

    Song

    Arrange, check and download

    Build a song

    Add sections in the order you want, or let the app arrange them for you. Then download it.

    Sound check–

      Audio is a finished recording (WAV) you can play anywhere. MIDI files hold only the notes, so you can open them in GarageBand, FL Studio or any music app and pick your own sounds. You get a .zip with the whole band in one file, plus one file per instrument.

      Advanced: save, load and copy

      Back up your whole project as a file, load one back, or copy the chord chart as text.

      Your work is kept on this device. No sound on iPhone? Turn off silent mode.
