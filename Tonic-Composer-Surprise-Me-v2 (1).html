        Quality lab

        Eight checks a computer can prove: chord names match the notes, notes are in key, the tune lands on chord tones, nothing rubs, every instrument stays in range, the tune moves smoothly, chords fit the mode, and bars are not copies. They prove the rules hold. Whether it moves you is for your ears.

        How Tonic works

        1. Pick a vibe. Open Compose, choose a mood and a style, then tap New song from this vibe.
        2. Tap what you want to change. Tap a part or a chord in the Flow map. Generate only changes what you picked, and the box above it says exactly what will happen.
        3. Lock what you love. The lock keeps a chord or a part safe while you keep generating.
        4. Shape it. Energy, Busy, Fancy and Loose make it calmer or livelier. Press and hold a chord for flavors, flips and swaps.
        5. Bring your own. Import chords from a MIDI file, or use a ready-made move from the Moves library. Edit notes lets you change a melody by hand.
        6. Take it with you. Export MIDI or audio from the Song tab. Your work is saved on this device.
